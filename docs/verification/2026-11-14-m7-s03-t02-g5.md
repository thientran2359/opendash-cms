## Implemented Changes

- `cms/crates/opendash-server/src/document.rs` & `main.rs`: bounded single-pass `normalize_public_document` (`<= 4 MiB`), handling mixed-case wrappers, body attributes, comments/quoted delimiters, `<style>` raw-text termination, stripping forged `<title>`/`<meta>`/`<link rel="canonical">`, and rejecting duplicate envelopes, `<script>`/`<object>`/`<base>`, event handlers, and unclosed tags/raw-text.
- `cms/crates/opendash-server/src/seo.rs`, `error.rs`, `storage/seo.rs`: `SeoMetadataInput`, `EntrySeoSnapshot`, `HomeSeoSnapshot`, `SeoView`, `validate_seo_input` (`422` on malformed input), `get_entry_seo`, `put_entry_seo` (ENTRY CAS), `get_home_seo`, `put_home_seo` (Home CAS), `sync_entry_seo_media_ref_in_tx`, `build_entry_seo_view`, `build_home_seo_view`, `build_simple_seo_view`.
- `cms/crates/opendash-server/src/storage/entries.rs` & `storage/public_content.rs`: atomic `seo_public_media_refs` sync on publish/unpublish/delete, and `media_is_referenced` inclusion for published routable entries and saved Home SEO.
- `cms/crates/opendash-server/src/content_render.rs`, `routes/{seo,public,public_content,entries}.rs`, `app.rs`: replaced `compose_public_document` with `compose_document_with_seo` across Home, archive, taxonomy, single, fallback 404, and entry preview (`noindex,nofollow` + `private, no-store`), plus `GET/PUT /api/admin/content-types/{type_key}/entries/{id}/seo` (Editor/Admin) and `GET/PUT /api/admin/seo/home` (Admin-only, `403` for Editor).
- `cms/admin/src/api/seo.ts`, `api/index.ts`, `features/seo/SeoPanel.tsx`, `features/content/ContentWorkspace.tsx`, `App.tsx`: Home SEO panel in Settings (with `412` draft preservation) and Entry Route & SEO card in ContentWorkspace.
- `cms/crates/opendash-server/tests/m7_s03_t02_seo.rs`: end-to-end integration test covering Editor vs Admin RBAC (`403` on Home SEO), `422` malformed SEO payloads, XSS escaping, `public_origin` canonical (no Host fallback), preview `noindex` + `private, no-store`, OG media public ACL across draft/publish/unpublish, Home SEO CAS (`412`/`200`), and `404` `noindex`.
